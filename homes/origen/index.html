Origen has a family. It is no longer available.

We are sharing it here as a record of our work. For a similar brief, please reach out about one of our other homes.

A low house kept behind its pines, reached on foot through the garden.

THE LANDSCAPE

The pines stayed. The slope between them was replanted among the rocks, and a timber walkway crosses it to a deck that looks back at the house and out to the sea.

THE ARCHITECTURE

A single-storey white house that opens onto a lawn, set back among the trees.

THE MATERIALS

Timber decking, rope balustrades and gravel paths. Sources and makers to be confirmed by SADALA before publication.

The Legacy Book

The story, handed over with the keys.

Origen was delivered with its Legacy Book: the story of the land, the architects who shaped it, the hands that made it, and the small moments that marked its building. A record that stays with the home.
